# Task Plan
## Steps
1. [ ] Identify the specific meeting for which minutes are required (date, attendees, agenda).
2. [ ] Gather all available raw notes, recordings, or other relevant information from the meeting.
3. [ ] Draft the meeting minutes, including attendees, discussions, decisions, action items (with owners and deadlines), and next steps.
4. [ ] Review and edit the draft for accuracy, completeness, clarity, and grammatical correctness.
5. [ ] Distribute the finalized meeting minutes to all relevant attendees and stakeholders.
